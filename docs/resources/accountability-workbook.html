The 8-Week Accountability Challenge

one small challenge a week, and big change over time

Jennifer's own health turnaround started with ten minutes in a chair. This workbook follows the same idea: set meaningful goals, build positive habits, and grow your mindset, one doable step at a time.

Your 8 weeks
  1. Goal setting & daily rituals
  2. Breaking mindless habits
  3. Daily journaling
  4. Lifelong learning
  5. Hydration awareness
  6. Mindfulness & meditation
  7. Reflect & revise
  8. Integration & moving forward
How to use this workbook
  • Have a pen ready. Writing it down makes it real.
  • Come with an open mind and curiosity.
  • Find an accountability buddy and share your wins.
  • Be gentle with yourself. Progress, not perfection.
  • Celebrate every week, out loud.
SMART goals

Specific: be precise about what you want. · Measurable: make it trackable. · Attainable: keep it realistic. · Relevant: align it with your values. · Timely: give it a deadline.

Lifelong Learning

Week5

Hydrate!

Aim for at least 64 ounces (eight 8-oz glasses) of water a day. Coffee doesn't count.

Our bodies are about 60% water. Even mild dehydration can bring fatigue, irritability, and fuzzy focus. Feeling cranky or tired? Drink a big glass of water and check in with yourself 30 minutes later.

Week6

Mindfulness & Meditation

Ten minutes a day of meditation or deep breathing to clear your mind and sharpen your focus.

Try it: box breathing
Breathe in for 4 · hold for 4 · breathe out for 4 · pause for 4. Repeat. It shifts your body from “fight or flight” to “rest and digest.”
Tips
  • Create a calm, comfy spot
  • Start with 5–10 minutes
  • Release expectations, because it's a skill
  • Begin each session with deep breaths
